📖 About Us

HppDuanju is a premium vertical short drama online streaming platform designed specifically for young audiences. We have gathered a massive collection of popular short dramas covering various genres, including fantasy, urban suspense, underdog counterattacks, campus youth, and sweet romance.

Our platform focuses on the seamless experience of "free online short dramas, instant playback, episode after episode." Whether you are on a PC browser or a mobile device, you can enjoy an immersive binge-watching experience anytime, anywhere.

✨ Features

📱 Multi-Platform Compatibility: Supports Android APP downloads. iPhone and PC users can watch seamlessly directly via the web version. Viewing progress is automatically synchronized across multiple devices.

🎬 Massive Collection, Daily Updates: Handpicked high-quality theater content. From male-lead counterattacks to strong female-lead stories and brain-burning suspense, there is always one that will get you "hooked."

⚡ Instant Play, Vertical Immersion: Optimized vertical playback for mobile. Simply swipe up to switch to the next drama smoothly and effortlessly.

🤝 Co-Creation & Deep Interaction: A unique "Plot Co-Creation" feature! Viewers can participate in plot discussions initiated by creators, offer ideas, and vote. Highly-voted suggestions have a chance to be written directly into the script.

🎯 What We Solve

Fragmented Entertainment Needs: Each episode is short, fast-paced, and highly engaging, perfectly filling fragmented time like commuting, lunch breaks, or waiting in line.

Hard to Find Good Shows: The platform provides detailed category tags, "Homepage Picks," and "Watchlist" functions to help you quickly discover high-quality dramas.

Device Restrictions: No tedious installation required. iOS and PC users can simply open the webpage to get a smooth experience akin to a native App.

❓ FAQ

Q1: Are the dramas on HppDuanju free?

The free scope is subject to the platform's markings. The platform contains a large amount of completely free content. Some special episodes can be unlocked by claiming coins through daily check-ins, or by subscribing to VIP for exclusive privileges.

Q2: Can I watch an entire drama on the webpage?

Absolutely. Free episodes can be watched directly; after logging in by scanning a QR code with the HppDuanju App, you can also seamlessly unlock, recharge, and watch VIP content on the web with the exact same experience.

Q3: Can I watch it on an iPhone?

Yes! iPhone users do not need to download an extra App. Simply open the web version in your browser for smooth streaming, perfectly adapted to iOS.

Q4: How does "Plot Co-Creation" work?

The creators will start topic discussions about plot directions in the community or comment sections. You can post your imaginative ideas and suggestions. If your idea gets the most likes, there is a high probability it will be filmed in subsequent episodes!

🛠️ Troubleshooting

If you experience slow loading or cannot open hppduanju.com, please try the following steps:

Clear Cache: Clear your browser's cache and cookies, then reload the page.

Switch Networks: Try switching from Wi-Fi to mobile data (4G/5G) or change to a different network environment.

Check Network Settings: Ensure your network environment is standard. Certain proxies or VPNs may affect video loading.

Change Browser: We recommend using mainstream browsers like Chrome, Edge, or Safari for optimal compatibility.

Watch this Repo: In case of sudden platform maintenance, we will promptly post announcements in the Issues or README of this GitHub repository.
